3. Customer support reply templates

Problem: slow, inconsistent replies. What I did: wrote reusable, polite replies for common situations. Tools: plain-text templates. Fill in the [brackets] for each customer.

Order status

Hi [Name], thank you for your message! Your order [#] is currently [status] and is expected to arrive by [date]. You can track it anytime using [tracking link]. Please let me know if you need anything else.

Return request

Hi [Name], I’m sorry the item didn’t work out. To return it, please reply with your order number and a photo of the item. Once I receive these, I’ll send the return steps within [timeframe].

Delivery delay

Hi [Name], I apologize for the delay with order [#]. It’s now scheduled for [new date]. Thank you for your patience, and I’ll update you as soon as it ships.
